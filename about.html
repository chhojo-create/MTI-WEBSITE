Who We Are

MTI at a Glance

Mina Textile Industries (MTI) is an ISO 9001:2015-certified company, founded in 1964, which has grown from supplying raw textile castings to delivering precision-engineered power transmission products and industrial castings for OEMs worldwide.

Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, MTI combines decades of expertise with modern manufacturing facilities to serve diverse sectors including construction, agriculture, and energy. Our fully integrated in-house manufacturing — from induction melting to CNC machining and finishing — ensures cost-effectiveness, rapid turnaround, and stringent quality control.

Where We Stand

Market Position

India's foundry and precision engineering sector is experiencing strong tailwinds — rapid industrialization, global supply chain diversification, and supportive government policies like Make in India and PLI schemes.

India is the second-largest casting producer globally, and Gujarat is one of its most vibrant industrial clusters. MTI is ideally positioned to scale and serve both domestic and international markets as a consistently reliable, customer-centric manufacturer.

Environmental Responsibility

Sustainability
at MTI

Sand Reclamation System

Our foundry recycles and reuses moulding sand, reducing raw material consumption and minimising industrial waste generation.

Energy-Efficient Furnaces

Modern induction furnaces significantly reduce energy consumption during metal melting, lowering carbon emissions and power dependency.

Low-VOC Coatings

We prioritise low-VOC powder coating and CED painting processes to reduce air pollution and improve safety for workers and the community.

Solid Waste Management

Structured waste segregation at source, safe disposal of industrial scrap, and recycling initiatives in collaboration with certified vendors.
